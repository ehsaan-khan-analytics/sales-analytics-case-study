# Sales Analytics Case Study

## Sales Growth, Margin Quality and Commercial Decision-Making
![Sales Analytics portfolio cover](Ehsaan-Khan-Sales-Analytics-Cover.png)

This independent portfolio project examines whether revenue growth is translating into profitable growth—and where pricing, promotion or returns activity should change.

The case study combines commercial analysis, channel segmentation, reporting automation and visible data-quality controls.

[**View the complete Sales Analytics case study (PDF)**](Ehsaan-Khan-Sales-Analytics-Case-Study.pdf)

> **Portfolio disclosure:** This project uses entirely synthetic data and contains no employer or customer information. Financial benefits are illustrative planning scenarios, not realised outcomes.

## Business Question

**Which channels are generating revenue without converting it into margin, and where should pricing or promotional activity change?**

The intended audience is sales leadership, commercial finance and channel owners.

## Initial Trading Picture

* Net sales increased by **8.2% year on year**.
* Orders increased by **11.4% year on year**.
* Gross margin fell by **1.8 percentage points**.
* The return rate rose by **0.7 percentage points**.
* Direct sales produced a **35.2% margin**, compared with **29.4% for resellers** and **24.1% for marketplace sales**.

The topline showed growth, but the channel view revealed weaker margin quality.

## Priority Segment

The clearest commercial issue appeared in:

**Marketplace → Audio Accessories → North**

* Gross margin was **22.4%**, 9.2 percentage points below the overall portfolio.
* The return rate reached **9.8%**, approximately twice the portfolio average.
* The segment represented **14.6% of sales** but only **6.3% of gross profit**.
* Discount-led growth was therefore adding revenue without producing a proportionate profit contribution.

## Recommended Business Response

I would take a focused commercial response:

1. Introduce and monitor a minimum margin floor.
2. Investigate the product-listing and returns issues within the segment.
3. Review discount exceptions before approving additional promotions.
4. Correct the operational causes of returns before purchasing further growth.
5. Track sales, margin, returns and gross-profit contribution together.

A scenario model indicated a potential **£38,000 gross-profit uplift** if segment margin improved by three percentage points. This is an illustrative scenario and not a realised result.

## Reporting Automation

The proposed weekly reporting workflow reduces repetitive handling while retaining analyst review.

| ---------------------------- | ---------------------: |

Human judgement remains at margin checks, exception investigation, commercial commentary and action recommendations.

## Control Framework

| Control                | Purpose                                                 |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| Order deduplication    | Confirm each order ID appears once                      |
| Returns reconciliation | Match posted returns to recorded sales                  |
| Discount exceptions    | Flag transactions priced below the agreed floor         |
| Margin bridge          | Explain movements between sales growth and gross margin |
